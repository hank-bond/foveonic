# Photographic finishing

Camera recipes must retain sensor calibration, corrections, noise cleanup, reconstruction and base camera appearance. Photographic operations with matching numerical meanings should be reusable across architectures. Existing methods and defaults must remain available while the owner evaluates alternatives.

Universal tone must offer consistent contrast and saturation independent of the camera tone implementation. Positive saturation should restrain already colorful samples. Negative saturation should remain ordinary desaturation. RAW exposure retains its existing interpretation. The approved spatial [HDR response](./tonal-ranges.md) must become available across photographic architectures. The tone-method choice must not switch HDR algorithms or move the camera adapter's HDR boundary. Classic, Merrill and Quattro, including Tiger, must share a linear photographic-edit stage before their camera tone response. Each camera adapter supplies primaries, nominal white, working geometry and the camera-curve-dependent HDR pivot. Required sensor calibration and reconstruction remain camera-owned. DNG baking follows the shared boundary below. Existing clipping before that boundary cannot be recovered by later edits.

Across Classic, Merrill and Quattro, Reference tone must expose the original Shadows and Highlights, and bypass the four HDR ranges and shared Local Contrast. Universal must expose Blacks, Shadows, Highlights, Whites and Local Contrast, and bypass the original Shadows and Highlights. Exposure, contrast and saturation remain common. Method changes retain the inactive values without applying hidden edits. This choice does not change either HDR implementation or its processing position.

Monochrome must retain its authored layer mixture and reconstruction. Red, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue and Magenta sensitivity controls must identify subject colors from an independent white-balanced color guide before monochrome mixing and creative saturation. Brightness adjustments apply to the resulting monochrome image. Color and Monochrome share this response on all supported architectures. Hue transitions must be smooth and nearly achromatic pixels protected. Zero sensitivity must avoid the guide branch. Guide generation must reuse the normal source and rendering operations, remain independent of the selected monochrome mixture, and participate in bounded interactive reuse.

Color and Monochrome must expose the same six sensitivity controls and use the same pre-tone scalar brightness response. Classification and spatial stabilization must both use an independent cleaned neutral color guide, so the gain is independent of the monochrome mixture and retained creative edits. Apply one gain to all RGB components, preserving their ratios, signed samples and headroom. Full, reduced and viewport rendering must use matching guide geometry and sufficient filter support. Neutral settings must skip guide construction and response dispatches.

All supported Color and Monochrome recipes must apply sensitivity, spatial HDR, Local Contrast and selected Universal sharpening in that order before the camera tone response. DNG must bake their active combined scalar brightness gain into its existing technical samples. Derive that gain from the same selected-look linear recipe used by native photographic rendering, including the camera-curve-dependent HDR pivot. Transfer only brightness gain, retaining the established DNG technical color basis and camera appearance metadata. The Tone and Color bypass suppresses sensitivity, HDR and Local Contrast. The Sharpening bypass suppresses its selected method in display and export. Neutral controls skip their work. Preserve the original X3F and editing sidecar for changing baked adjustments. DNG is an edited derivative, not an X3F replacement. Global tone presentation retains its existing DNG policy. Reference sharpening retains each camera recipe’s technical boundary. Universal sharpening must replace its creative contribution and bake into DNG through the shared linear stage. Required Quattro reconstruction and its unsigned-code limits remain in force.

Sharpening must offer Reference and Universal methods on all families. Universal must replace reference creative sharpening contributions and offer Amount, Radius, Threshold and Halo suppression. Necessary reconstruction remains independent. Neutral-centered multiscale Local Contrast is an accepted standalone photographic effect. It must remain distinct from denoising, reconstruction detail and sharpening, rather than replace any of them.

Quattro Smooth/Crisp combines residual restoration, local contrast and adaptive sharpening. Shared Local Contrast must not be presented as an equivalent reconstruction method. The owner accepted its distinct look after the comparison. Reference remains the default method. Its sharpening amount defaults to Auto when no amount is saved. Explicit saved manual amounts remain unchanged. Universal sharpening uses manual controls without Auto. Its pre-curve response requires photographic review separately from the retained Reference default.

All pixel operations run on the GPU through the existing typed graph and executor. Inspection, per-image persistence, section bypasses, frozen batch requests, Fast and Smart must share the same settings. Spatial operations must use sufficient regional support or explicitly run over a larger frame. Neutral operations must skip their work and preserve samples. Retained outputs remain immutable.

Remove redundant export-format labels from section headers now that both DNG and TIFF support the sections. Help must explain the difference between baked linear edits and embedded global presentation. Sharpening uses Method and Amount labels. Highlight correction and its Threshold belong in one parameter group. Indented text must use half the previous gap after its pipe without changing checkbox indentation.

All UI slider readouts, including sensitivity and hover values, must show at most three significant digits while retaining full stored precision. Formatting and numeric-field sizing must agree so value changes do not expand the editor panel. Very small or large magnitudes may use compact scientific notation.

Review must cover portraits, hair, fabric, foliage, sky and shadows. Validate arithmetic, finite headroom, neutral/reference output, cached/fresh agreement, regional agreement, TIFF inclusion and the documented DNG baking boundary. Report measured responsiveness and remaining visual decisions before selecting new defaults.
