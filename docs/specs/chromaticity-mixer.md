# Chromaticity mixer

The Color Mix control must show the CIE 1931 xy spectral-locus horseshoe with fixed representative red, green and blue anchors on its boundary. A dashed triangle joins the anchors and limits selection to valid nonnegative layer mixtures, including pure-layer corners. Dragging, arrow keys, labeled proportions and double-click reset must remain available.

The triangle provides a geometric guide for the existing three proportions. Application display-gamut settings must not affect this control or its saved weights. Merrill sensor layers overlap spectrally. The anchors and approximate background colors are an illustration rather than measured sensor primaries, a chosen output gamut or a chromaticity readout. The spectral locus must retain its source attribution and license.
