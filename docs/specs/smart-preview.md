# Smart preview

The viewport preview selector must offer Smart for supported Merrill, ordinary Quattro and Tiger photographs. The application-wide preference must remain visible while loading. Resolve unavailable Fast/Smart choices from early capture metadata, showing Full and disabling the other modes without replacing the stored preference. Full metadata may refine that availability before it is used for subsequent requests. Classic Smart remains future work. Explicit Full, Fast, Draft and Thumb retain their selected processing level through zoom and selection changes. Existing Full and Fast processing remains available. The initial workspace choice remains Full unless the owner selects another preference.

Smart must choose the coarsest of the existing Full, Fast, Draft and Thumb levels that requires no more than 1.5 physical display pixels per rendered sample in either dimension. This provisional enlargement limit is subject to visual review. Resolve each photograph independently from its displayed image geometry, excluding letterboxing and captions. Count neither browser-selected photos nor editing targets as a substitute for pixel demand. Use native composition coordinates even when a final output resize is requested. Camera-specific Thumb sizing remains governed by [small previews](./small-previews.md). Until source geometry is known, Fast may serve as a temporary request. Geometry arriving, viewport resizing, display-density changes and deliberate zoom must update the requested level only when the effective choice changes. A compatible completed finer image may remain visible without rerendering unchanged pixels. Subsequent photographic edits use the currently requested level. When Smart requests native detail after deliberate zoom with a compatible previous image and known viewport geometry, retain that image while rendering at Full quality. Render the visible region first when supported, then prepare whole-frame Full as a background continuation after all currently requested visible renders. When the requested recipe requires whole-photo processing, request whole-frame Full directly. Adaptive base response may use its reduced whole-photo analysis while native application processes the visible region. Each overview must honor requested Noise, Detail and ColorDQ controls, using the same recipe and reduced working-set owner as its corresponding explicit quality. Native detail reacquires original decoded input through the source loader. Reduced overviews do not imply a native-RAM retention guarantee. Smart must request Full processing when displayed demand exceeds the permitted enlargement of Fast. Quattro, Tiger and [Merrill](./merrill-fast-preview.md) must process the active viewport and necessary neighboring samples first, then prepare whole-frame Full after current foreground rendering has settled. Recipes may include complete native processing tiles around the viewport. Explicit output resizing may fall back to direct whole-frame refinement where regional sampling is unsupported. Panning must not queue more regional renders. Until the whole frame completes, retain the compatible previous image and any completed detail tile at its original coordinates. Once Full completes, it may be reused throughout the image, including after returning to Fit, until changed edits require processing at the newly resolved level. Changing source or edits resets this sequence. Outdated results must not replace the current source or requested edits.

Mandatory sparse sensor corrections must run in native sensor coordinates before reduced-preview averaging, including in the Fast overview. Use each camera's own gain tables, defect maps and geometry. Requested noise or detail bypass must not expose phase-detection or low-sensitivity sensor sites in the overview.

Rapid edits must retain the latest completed compatible image, including a regional detail tile, while the next image renders. Changing a slider value must not expose an older base image by hiding its newer tile. A newer completed whole image must supersede older detail even when another edit is pending. Compatibility requires matching source, inspection root, rendition, calibration and framing. Older completed results may advance feedback without changing the requested editing values.

Warm regional edits and their whole-frame Full continuations must retain reusable inputs across the handoff within the shared interactive cache budget. They must not rerun unchanged reconstruction just because their processing grids differ. Measure the combined slice and Full completion, including display preparation, against an approximately 90 ms target for warm exposure edits. The owner accepts modest variation with GPU load and image differences. Source loading, initial grid preparation, scopes and physical screen presentation are separate measurements.

Whole-frame statistics and operations with global dependencies may remain whole-frame. Reuse their results where valid, and restrict later operations to the requested region where their contracts allow. Cropping a completed full render alone does not satisfy regional-processing support. The recipe must own region geometry, alignment and required margins. Shared numerical operations remain independent of camera identity and GUI policy.

The overview and detail must share native crop, orientation and viewport coordinates. Scopes and navigator must keep their established source/edit pairing. Inspection, comparisons and exports remain Full. Supported monochrome camera adapters may render native detail regionally. Displayed regions and reduced overview buffers must remain immutable while newer work runs. Reduced/full appearance differences accepted for Fast also apply during Smart refinement.

Crop and rotation are composition changes over completed photographic pixels. Entering, leaving or committing their shared editing plane must keep the current working resolution and reuse compatible whole-frame pixels. Fitting new framing must not trigger detail promotion. Explicit zoom and photographic edits retain the refinement rules above. Composition-only requests must not cancel matching in-flight photographic work or publish pixels from another source, rendition, inspection root or calibration.

Embedded previews must use the smallest suitable camera thumbnail before rendering. The native photograph geometry must remain consistent with the Raw output and orientation. Version-4 header dimensions, separate JPEG thumbnails and bounded Exif thumbnails may provide this framing without sensor decoding. Remove confirmed camera thumbnail padding. Differently cropped camera JPEGs must not define Raw viewport coordinates. Whole-image scopes and the navigator follow the complete image. Viewport scopes may use regional detail when it covers the complete visible area.

## Refinement ownership

Every displayed photograph must resolve Smart refinement from its own viewport, effective settings and compatible whole image. Zooming or editing one photograph must preserve the other photographs' requested resolutions and unfinished refinements. Current detail and continuation follow the complete demanded photo set across active-selection and Before changes. Removing demand cancels unfinished work while bounded retained history may keep completed pixels. Camera, Defaults, inspection, scopes and picks retain their active-photo lifetimes.

Current detail belongs to the existing bounded per-photo image owner, including detail for the active photo. Camera and Defaults detail belongs to Before. Detail memory must participate in the owner's existing accounting, eviction and clear behavior. Presentation-only selection or Before restoration must preserve rendered content ordering without changing diagnostic request identity. A newer whole image supersedes an older detail regardless of presentation rebasing or a later pending edit. A detail request that falls back to whole-image coverage must retain its detail purpose and must not replace the main preview or seed browser previews.

The UI must publish photo/settings, visible image coordinates and required resolution without sequencing rendering from paint updates or global worker-busy state. The render coordinator owns each requested refinement and its optional whole-image continuation. It handles regional completion and publishes the continuation independently of UI delivery and unrelated photo requests. A request without continuation settles after the regional result. Existing Smart interactions request continuation.

Successful current regional results must retain their delivery decision before continuation replaces desired work. Errors, superseded requests, changed sources and cancelled requests must not start obsolete continuation. If the first result already covers the whole native image, the coordinator must not render it again. UI request revisions reserve the regional and continuation order. The processing engine continues to own regional support and numerical cache validity.

## Continuation priority

Automatic Whole continuation is speculative preparation for later panning. All user-requested visible renders take priority, including visible renders for other photographs. New foreground demand must stop further continuation operations at an available processing boundary. Already-submitted GPU work may complete first. A yielded continuation remains pending while its source and settings are current, then may resume using valid checkpoints after foreground work settles. Superseded or removed demand is retired normally. Explicit user-requested Whole renders remain foreground work.
