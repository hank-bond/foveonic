    Full parameter sweep: numerical screening and color tradeoffs

    These measurements stop before ColorDQ. Lower green excess is not proof of correct scene color. The noisy-color fixtures deliberately contain a correct filtered color and an uncertain input. Their retention shows the risk of desaturating real color. Color retention is the fraction of original chroma: 1 is unchanged and 0 is neutral. Mean applied weight covers the full processing atlas, including halos. Screening uses the original study crops.     Outside-mask changes are checked at the native texture stage. Layer-weighted brightness and linear RGB luminance are measured separately. Atlas pixel counts include processing halos, so they are not unique photographic-pixel counts. Final tone and color appearance may still change at corrected pixels.
