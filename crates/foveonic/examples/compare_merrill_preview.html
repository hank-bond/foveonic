Fast omits row correction and Noise and Detail, then uses half width and height for camera color and finishing after native calibration, repairs, highlights and despeckle. The alternate Fast version also omits despeckle. Full applies the requested Noise and Detail controls. Use Fit to judge composition and color, or native 100% to inspect detail. Space alternates Full with your last Fast choice. Both versions keep the same framing.
