# Responsiveness campaign

## Owner direction

The owner authorized a broad optimization campaign covering startup, browsing, redundant processing, reusable analysis, operation ordering and Smart preview scheduling. The requested crop-first Smart experience concerns the visible region while zoomed in, not a saved photographic crop. Reduced-resolution Dehaze and Adaptive analysis may be investigated with explicit accuracy comparisons. Promising changes may be integrated on a new branch after review. Use parallel isolated worktrees and shared Rust dependency artifacts. Preserve existing unrelated edits.

The coordinator assigns work, reviews simplicity and architectural fit, and records successes and failures here. Terra 6.1 handles initial investigations and implementation. Sol 6.1 is available for difficult reviews. This direction reopens the broader optimization scope previously closed in the [editing handoff](../editing-optimization-handoff.md).

The owner subsequently requested autonomous continuation while asleep until credible avenues have been investigated, a net improvement in reusable profiling tools, and small useful project-organization improvements. Do not depend on live answers for routine implementation choices. Evidence and unresolved limitations remain explicit.

For zoomed Smart preview, the owner prioritized useful Full-resolution regional detail over a slightly earlier low-resolution Fast frame, which is distracting at that scale. Implement direct Full-region rendering where supported, retain the current compatible image during processing, then continue whole-image Full. Timing characterizes this behavior rather than requiring it to beat Fast's first-image latency.

## Evaluation

Follow [profiling requirements](../../docs/specs/profiling.md), [profiling usage](../../docs/profiling.md), [interactive rendering](../../docs/specs/interactive-rendering.md) and each affected feature specification. Exact optimizations preserve output and cache identity. Approximate experiments need separate quality evidence before adoption. Preserve bounded ownership, newest-request scheduling, export fidelity and image provenance.

The owner explicitly permits heuristic optimizations with nonidentical output. Measure camera-code differences and visually inspect affected regions, primarily with crops. Small whole-frame aggregate error is insufficient. Record concentrated errors and inspect worst-error regions alongside representative edges, texture, dark regions and highlights. Explain the visible effect and retain the baseline for comparison. Do not describe an approximate result as exact.

GPU measurements run serially, with builds and memory sampling outside timed intervals. Native pointer runs require separate coordination. Use repository-local example photographs. Record recipe, fixture, timing boundary, warmup, repetitions and limitations. Do not present synthetic or engine-ready timings as native screen latency.

## Work organization

Integration branch: `optimization/responsiveness`, starting at `9b606c8`. Independent branches: `optimization/browsing`, `optimization/pipeline` and `optimization/smart-preview`, plus `optimization/neutral` for the final neutral-stage trial. Worktrees live under ignored `.local/worktrees/`. All use the original checkout's `target` directory through `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`. Cargo serializes writers to that directory. Research and edits remain parallel. Each benchmark executable must be copied to its trial directory before another branch rebuilds the same target. This shares dependency artifacts without introducing another cache service or multiplying target directories.
